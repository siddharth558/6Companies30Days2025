# 6-Companies 30-Days Challenge

Welcome to **Arsh Goyal's 6-Companies 30-Days Challenge**! This challenge is designed to help you master DSA (Data Structures and Algorithms) through curated questions from top companies like **Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Adobe, Flipkart, and Google**.

---

## Challenge Overview

- **Duration**: 30 Days  
- **Companies Covered**:  
  1. Microsoft  
  2. Amazon  
  3. Goldman Sachs  
  4. Adobe  
  5. Flipkart  
  6. Google  

- **Goal**: Solve company-specific DSA problems within 5 days for each company.

---

## Challenge Structure

### 📅 Week 1: Microsoft  
Solve DSA problems asked in **Microsoft** interviews.

### 📅 Week 2: Amazon  
Focus on questions frequently appearing in **Amazon** coding interviews.

### 📅 Week 3: Goldman Sachs  
Tackle problems related to **Goldman Sachs**' coding rounds.

### 📅 Week 4: Adobe  
Work through questions based on **Adobe** technical interviews.

### 📅 Week 5: Flipkart  
Solve problems from **Flipkart**'s coding challenges.

### 📅 Week 6: Google  
Finally, test your skills with **Google**'s DSA questions.

---

## Benefits of the Challenge

1. **Targeted Practice**: Focused problem sets from top companies.  
2. **Improved Problem-Solving**: Strengthen your logical and analytical skills.  
3. **Enhanced Confidence**: Prepare for technical interviews with better clarity.  
4. **Time-Management Skills**: Solve problems within the stipulated time.

---

## How to Participate

1. **Commit 1-2 Hours Daily**: Dedicate time every day to solving these problems.  
2. **Track Your Progress**: Maintain a checklist of solved problems.  
3. **Analyze Solutions**: Learn from both your correct and incorrect attempts.
